# Brand Brief

> Captured: 2026-08-14
> Update this file as the business evolves.

## Business
A powersports dealership selling TaoTao and Tank motoscooters, plus an assortment of motorcycles and mini-bikes.

## Customer
Customers come from many walks of life — commuters saving money, outdoor/riding enthusiasts looking to relax and unwind, teens and younger kids who love riding, and families who ride together. The biggest single group, and the one who buys most often, is young people who need affordable, economical daily transportation and want a scooter that's cheap to run day to day.

## Primary CTA
Buy a scooter.

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
We give customers the full picture of what to expect when owning a scooter — the pros AND the cons, not just the sales pitch. We let them know we'll be there for them through the whole ride (not just the sale). Most shops sell and move on; we stay in it with the customer.

## Story Vault
- The #1 question customers ask is "how fast does it go?" — but the real answer depends on who's asking: a parent buying for their kid wants it NOT too fast, while an adult buying for themselves wants it fast enough to keep up with traffic.

## Voice
Warm and personal, professional and clean.

## Universal Voice Rules (apply to every post)
- Contractions always ("don't" not "do not")
- Active voice, short sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Numbers as digits ("3 tips" not "three tips")
- No em dashes — use commas, periods, or "..."
- One concrete idea per post, not three
- Specific details over generic statements
