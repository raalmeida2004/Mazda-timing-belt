Our Story

Born from a passion for the open road and a love of two-wheeled freedom, RUNAMOKMOTO has been outfitting riders across New England since day one. We're not just a dealership — we're riders who happen to sell scooters.

Why Choose Us

Every scooter on our lot is hand-picked for quality, reliability, and pure riding joy. From first-time commuters to weekend warriors, we match every rider to the right machine — no upsells, no pressure, just honest advice.

Test Rides Welcome

We encourage you to come by, meet the team, and take a spin. Because the best way to fall in love with a scooter is to actually ride one. Give Rick a call and he'll have one warmed up and waiting.

Service & Support
